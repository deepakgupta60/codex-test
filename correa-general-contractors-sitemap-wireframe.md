## 6) Visual/UX Direction (Premium + Engineered + Welcoming)

- **Grid system:** strict 12-column structure for an engineered feel
- **Whitespace:** generous spacing to communicate premium quality
- **Color palette:** charcoal, white, steel gray, one warm accent (e.g., muted copper)
- **Typography pairing:** modern sans-serif headline + highly legible body font
- **Imagery:** real project photography over generic stock where possible
- **Microinteractions:** subtle hover states, smooth reveal-on-scroll, no flashy effects

This structure balances authority, emotional trust, and lead conversion while staying true to a minimalist, premium brand presence in the Dallas construction market.
